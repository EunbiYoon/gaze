3. How it fails

Most failures stop in the wrong place or right next to the target

286 held-out images; the policy is run greedily from every level-0 start. The four parts sum to 100%.

near-miss = stopped in a cell adjacent to the target · distractor = stopped more than 1 cell away · timeout = did not stop within 24 steps. hacked (stopped in a cell whose training reward exceeds the target cell’s) is a subset of distractor: oracle 0.0%, clip_full 5.2%, clip_full_cgl 5.1%.
